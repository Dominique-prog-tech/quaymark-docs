# On-call duty

Vessels also arrive at night and at weekends. Per office someone is always **on call**: a 24/7 rota, in the office's time.

## Opening the screen

Click **On-call duty** in the menu on the left and choose the office. At the top you see who is on call now. Viewing requires the right to view the schedule; changing requires the right to schedule.

## Generating the rota

1. Click **Generate rota**.
2. In **Surveyors, in order**, choose the surveyors in rota order.
3. Choose the **First handover** (for example Monday 08:00), the **Days per block** (7 for a week) and the **Number of blocks**.
4. Leave **Replace existing blocks in this period** ticked to overwrite an old rota. A block that runs across the boundary of the new rota stays; Quaymark then reports the overlap.

Each block starts where the previous one ends. When summer or winter time starts, the handover stays at the same hour.

## Changing blocks

- **New block** adds a single block (surveyor, from, to, note).
- Double-click a block to change it, for example to swap with a colleague, or to delete it.

## Alerts

For the next four weeks Quaymark reports:

- a **gap**: a period without anyone on call;
- an on-call surveyor who is **absent** (leave, sickness, training) on one of the days of the block; → [Schedule](planning.md)
- **two on-call duties at once**, often a typing error.

The home screen shows per office who is on call now and how many alerts the rota has. The schedule shows the on-call blocks in the surveyor's column; they cannot be dragged there.
