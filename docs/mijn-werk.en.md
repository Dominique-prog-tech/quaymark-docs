# My work

The page of a **freelance surveyor**. With the Freelancer role, this is the only screen you see in the web app: your files, your time, your expenses and your statements. You do the on-site work in the field app. → [Freelancers](freelancers.md#the-freelancer-role)

## Opening the screen

Sign in: Quaymark opens **My work** straight away. In the menu it is listed as **My work**.

## My files

The files assigned to you, with their status as a coloured label (blue what is still to come, orange what is in progress, green what is done), the scheduled time and the port. You open them in the field app.

## My time

You record your time in the field app, on the file. Here you see everything you recorded, with the hours. What is on a statement is marked *Settled*.

## My expenses

Click **Add expense** for an expense you paid yourself (travel, parking, an overnight stay):

- **File**: a file assigned to you.
- **Date**, **Kind**, **Description** and **Amount**.
- **Receipt**: a photo or PDF, at most 15 MB. It is added to the attachments of the file, so the company sees it.

You can delete an expense that has not yet been settled or invoiced with **⋯** at the end of the row. After that it is fixed. Each list has a search box, and **Export** takes it to Excel.

If the company has not yet created your freelancer record, the page tells you. You can already enter expenses; settling is only possible with a record.

## My statements

The statements the company issued on your behalf (self-billing), with number, date, period and total. Click **PDF** to open them.
