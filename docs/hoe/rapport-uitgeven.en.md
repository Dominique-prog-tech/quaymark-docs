# How do I issue a report and deliver it to the principal?

1. **Set the file to the status Reporting.** From then on, the required survey fields must be filled in. → [File](../dossiers.md#file)
2. **Write the sections** on the **Report** tab, with your office's standard texts or a **draft** proposed by the AI, and click **Save sections**. → [Report](../dossiers.md#report) · [Propose a draft](../dossiers.md#propose-a-draft)
3. **Review the report** with **Report (PDF)**. Nothing is saved or sent yet. → [Report preview](../dossiers.md#report-preview)
4. **Click Issue report.** The report becomes version 1, with its fingerprint, and never changes after that. → [Issuing and sending the report](../dossiers.md#issuing-and-sending-the-report)
5. **Click Send.** The principal receives the PDF; the email is recorded in the file's journal.
6. **Need to correct something?** Use **Issue new version** and give a reason. The previous version is kept.
