# How do I do a draft survey?

1. **Choose a survey type with Draft survey ticked.** The file then gets the **Draft survey** tab under **Calculations**. → [Survey types](../soorten-survey.md#survey-type)
2. **Open the initial survey** by clicking **Initial survey** in the table header. Enter the six drafts, the vessel and the water. → [Draft survey](../dossiers.md#draft-survey)
3. **Read the hydrostatic values** at the mean draft Quaymark shows: displacement, TPC, LCF and MTC. Enter the LCF as the table gives it and say where it is measured from.
4. **Choose the hydrometer** in the **Measuring instruments** block. Quaymark keeps it with the calibration of the day of the survey. → [Measuring instruments](../meetapparatuur.md#during-a-measurement)
5. **Do the same for the final survey** after loading or discharging. It takes over the vessel and the water of the initial survey.
6. **Read the cargo weight** at the top of the tab: the difference in net displacement between the initial and final surveys.
7. **Issue the draft survey report.** It is then fixed with its fingerprint, with the letters and certificates of the file and in the evidence package. → [Letters and certificates](../dossiers.md#letters-and-certificates)
