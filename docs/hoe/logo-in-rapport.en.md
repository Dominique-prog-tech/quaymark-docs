# How do I put our logo in the report?

1. **Open the templates** with **Settings** and **Templates**, and open the **general** template. It applies to every survey type. → [The list](../sjablonen.md#the-list)
2. **Choose the tab of a language** and click **Edit document**. → [Report template](../sjablonen.md#report-template)
3. **Add your logo and house style** to the Word document, and save with **Save as new version**.
4. **Repeat this for every language** you report in. The **Documents** column shows the current version per language.
5. **Check the result** in a file with **Report (PDF)**. → [Report preview](../dossiers.md#report-preview)

A template for one survey type takes precedence over the general one: add the logo there too.
