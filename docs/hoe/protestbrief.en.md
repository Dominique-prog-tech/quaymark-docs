# How do I write a letter of protest?

1. **Open the file** and, on the **Report** tab, click **New letter**. → [Letters and certificates](../dossiers.md#letters-and-certificates)
2. **Choose the letter of protest under Kind** and the addressee under **To**. The subject is already filled in with the vessel and the port.
3. **Adapt the text.** Replace every placeholder between [square brackets]: as long as one remains, the letter cannot be issued.
4. **Review the letter** with **Preview (PDF)**.
5. **Click Issue.** The letter is fixed with its fingerprint and is included in the evidence package. → [Evidence package](../dossiers.md#evidence-package)

The layout of the letter comes from its template. → [Templates](../sjablonen.md#the-list)
