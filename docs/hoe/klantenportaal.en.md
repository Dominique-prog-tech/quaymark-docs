# How do I give a principal access to the client portal?

1. **Open the relation**: click **Relations** and double-click the principal. → [Relation](../relaties.md#relation)
2. **If the person is not listed yet**, add them on the **Contacts** tab, with an email address.
3. **Open the Client portal tab**, choose the contact person and click **Invite**. → [Client portal](../relaties.md#client-portal)
4. **The contact person chooses a password** with the link in the email (valid for 7 days) and sets up two-step verification at the first sign-in. The status then becomes *Active*.
5. **Must access stop?** Click **Revoke access**.

What the contact person sees in the portal is on the Relations page. → [Client portal](../relaties.md#client-portal)
