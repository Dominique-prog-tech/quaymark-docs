# How do I create a new file?

1. **Open the list** with **Files** in the menu and click **New file**. → [The list](../dossiers.md#the-list)
2. **Does the assignment arrive by email or as a pdf?** Then first click **Read document**: Quaymark pre-fills the form. → [From a document](../dossiers.md#from-a-document)
3. **Fill in the instruction:** office, survey type, principal, surveyor and report language. → [File](../dossiers.md#file)
4. **Fill in place and planning:** vessel, port, terminal or berth and the scheduled moment. If the vessel isn't there yet, create it first. → [Vessels](../schepen.md#the-list)
5. **Save.** The file now gets its number; after that the office is fixed.
6. **Add the other parties involved** on the **Parties** tab, with their role and reference. → [File](../dossiers.md#file)
7. **On site,** the surveyor records findings and checklist answers in the field app; they appear in the file. → [Findings](../dossiers.md#findings)
