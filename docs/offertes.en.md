# Quotations

A quotation to a principal, in the principal's language. Once accepted it becomes a file, and its lines become that file's flat fee.

## Opening the screen

Click **Quotations** in the menu on the left. You see the quotations with their number, principal, survey type, vessel, validity, total and status, and for an accepted quotation the file that came from it. The status is draft, sent, accepted, declined or expired. The status is shown as a coloured label, as on the quotation itself and in the principal portal: green accepted, red declined, blue sent, grey draft or expired. Viewing requires the right to view invoices; drafting, sending and deciding require the right to edit invoices.

## Quotation

- **New quotation** creates a draft. Fill in the principal, the office, the survey type, the date and **Valid until** (thirty days by default), and optionally the vessel, the port, the reference and a description. The language follows the principal. The currency is EUR, USD or GBP, as on the file.
- **Vessel**: choose a vessel Quaymark already knows, or **New vessel…**. For a new vessel, fill in the **Vessel name**, the **Kind** (seagoing or inland) and the **IMO number** or **ENI number**. The field tells you right away whether the number is valid, and whether it already belongs to a vessel: Quaymark then uses that vessel. If the vessel is not known yet, leave it empty.
- **Line from the rates** prepares a line with the flat fee of the survey type, or one hour at the hourly rate: then fill in the number of estimated hours. A [framework agreement](relaties.md#framework-agreements) of the principal is taken into account. Lines can be added, changed and deleted freely while the quotation is a draft.
- **Preview (PDF)** shows the quotation as it will go out. The layout comes from the "Quotation" template. → [Templates](sjablonen.md)

## Sending

**Send** gives the quotation its number (for example O-HQ-2026-0001), creates the PDF with its fingerprint and announces the quotation to the principal's portal users. Fill in an address to also email the PDF; empty means the portal only. After that the lines are fixed, and the quotation shows when it was sent.

A new vessel without an IMO or ENI number stops the quotation: the principal can accept it in the portal, and then nobody adds a number any more. Enter the number, choose a known vessel, or leave the vessel empty.

## Accepting or declining

- The principal can accept or decline the quotation in the **principal portal**, as long as it is valid. → [Principal portal](relaties.md#principal-portal)
- If you received the answer by email or phone, click **Accept** or **Decline** yourself. With Decline you can give a reason; it then appears on the quotation.
- Accepting creates a **file** with status New, with the principal, the survey type, the vessel, the port and the reference. Quaymark uses a known vessel (by its number, or by a name that only one vessel carries); a new vessel gets a record with the number from the quotation. An older quotation with only a name Quaymark does not know becomes a file without a vessel; the name is then in a note in the file's **Journal**, and you choose or create the vessel on the file. You then choose the surveyor and the scheduling in the file.
- The quotation's lines are the file's **flat fee**: the invoice proposal puts them on the invoice, and says so.
- A quotation is decided only once. After **Valid until** it is expired and can no longer be accepted.
